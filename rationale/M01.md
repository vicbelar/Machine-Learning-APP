# Rationale · M01

**One choice of our group that I doubt:** using the model we trained on one adult for the children of PERLA.

**Why:** Our RMS threshold (37.2 mV) was right 82.8 % of the time on sessions S05 and S06, but all the data comes from one adult who can hold a steady contraction. A child with cerebral palsy has a different muscle size, more fat under the skin, less control of the force and sometimes spasticity, so the signal at rest and in movement will have other values. I think the number itself will not work for the children; what we can reuse is the method: a short recording with each child to find their own threshold, tested on another session.

**One source I found:** Merletti R, Cerone GL. Tutorial: surface EMG detection, conditioning and pre-processing: best practices. *J Electromyogr Kinesiol* 54:102440, 2020. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jelekin.2020.102440 (the amplitude of surface EMG depends on the subject, the tissue under the electrodes and their placement).

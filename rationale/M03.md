# Rationale · M03

**One choice of our group that I support, with one doubt:** the ESP32 sends the raw signal of the converter (`raw_mV`) next to the filtered one (`emg_mV`), and we wrote the cleaning rule (a sample at the limit of the converter, or more than 1 % of samples missing) before we looked at any data.

**Why:** in the filtered signal, a jump at the limit of the converter can be set to 0 by the artifact rejection, so the rule can only see saturation in the raw signal. Writing the rule first stops us from keeping only the trials that suit us. My doubt: the rule rejected 0 of 120 trials, but it did not catch the two odd recordings of S04 (`QUALITY-REPORT.md`). A poor electrode contact is neither saturation nor a gap, so next time we should write notes in each session and look at the signal at rest before we record.

**One source I found:** Merletti R. Standards for reporting EMG data. *J Electromyogr Kinesiol* 9(1):III–IV, 1999. https://isek.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/Standards-for-Reporting-EMG-Data.pdf
